Our story

Does this sound familiar?

You or someone you love is gluten-free and often left out at work, or when baking is shared with family and friends.

From the kitchen bench

There are a lot of people in New Zealand like me who are gluten-free, whether from coeliac disease or intolerance, and we all deserve brownies that actually taste good and easy to access.

That's why I started baking my own, on my kitchen bench. My home kitchen is 100% gluten-free, so everything is made in a space that's safe for others who need to avoid gluten.

The goal was simple, brownies that just happen to be gluten-free.

And yes, non-gluten-free people still give them the thumbs up.

The Brownie Bench promise

  • A 100% gluten free kitchen

    Not a shared bench, not a "we're careful" kitchen. Nothing containing gluten comes through the door, so there's no cross-contamination to worry about.

  • Baked fresh to order

    Nothing sits in a freezer waiting for you. Your brownies are baked the week they're sent, then cut, wrapped and boxed by hand.

  • Delivered NZ-wide

    Tracked courier to any address in New Zealand, packed to arrive in one piece. Rural delivery is no problem.

Questions

Are they really gluten free?

Yes. They're baked in a home kitchen that is 100% gluten free — no gluten-containing ingredients are kept or used in the space at all.

Are they safe for coeliacs?

The kitchen is fully gluten free and there's no cross-contamination from gluten baking. If you are coeliac and want to check specific ingredients or suppliers before ordering, please get in touch and we'll talk you through it.

What about other allergens?

The brownies contain dairy and egg, and some flavours contain nuts. Nuts are handled in the kitchen, so we can't guarantee a nut-free product. A full ingredient list for each flavour is available on request.

Can I mix flavours in one box?

Absolutely — that's the idea. Every box can be filled with any combination of the five flavours.

How long do they keep?

Best in the first few days, kept in an airtight container. They also freeze well, so you can keep some back for later.

Do you do corporate or event orders?

Yes — morning teas, gifting and events are all welcome. Get in touch with numbers and a date and we'll work it out.
